Prepare Your File Before You Order

Most print delays and disappointments come from the artwork file, not the printer. Check these before you upload.

  • Format: print-ready PDF is best. AI or EPS with fonts outlined also works. Avoid low-resolution JPEGs.
  • Bleed: extend backgrounds 3 mm past the trim edge so no white edge appears after cutting.
  • Safe area: keep text and logos at least 3 to 5 mm inside the trim line.
  • Colour: design in CMYK. Bright RGB screen colours can look duller in print.
  • Resolution: 300 dpi at final size for cards and brochures. Large banners can use lower resolution because they are viewed from a distance.
  • Fonts: convert text to outlines or embed fonts.
  • Proof-read: check names, numbers, phone numbers and spelling out loud before approving.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best banner for outdoor use in Singapore?

PVC banners are the usual outdoor choice because they cope with sun and rain. For exposed or windy spots, a mesh PVC banner lets air pass through. Roll-up banners are designed for indoor events.

What size is a standard name card in Singapore?

The most common size is about 90 x 54 mm. Check the exact size on the product page and keep a 3 mm bleed in your artwork.

What file format should I send?

A print-ready PDF is best. AI or EPS with fonts outlined also works. Avoid low-resolution JPEGs.

How many panels does a tri-fold brochure have?

Six panels, three on each side. A bi-fold has four panels.
